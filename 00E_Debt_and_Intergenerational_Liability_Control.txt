00E — Debt and Intergenerational Liability Control
American Gold Contract for Liberty (AGCL)
Status: Foundational Control
Scope: All AGCL-governed borrowing, debt instruments, guarantees, obligations, and future commitments


I. PURPOSE

This Control binds debt to time, ownership, and consent.

No generation may obligate another without disclosure.
No debt may exist without a defined end.
No liability may be imposed on the unborn by inertia.

Debt must die before citizens do.


II. GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

Debt is deferred authority.

If authority must expire, so must debt.
If consent is required today, it is required tomorrow.
Intergenerational obligation without consent is theft by time.


III. DEFINITIONS

Debt  
Any borrowed funds, issued credit, guarantee, bond, obligation, promise of future payment, or unfunded liability.

Intergenerational Liability  
Any obligation whose cost, risk, or repayment extends beyond the consenting generation.

Payer Class  
The defined group responsible for repayment.

Sunset  
The fixed termination date beyond which no obligation may exist.


IV. DEBT CREATION REQUIREMENTS

Rule E1 — Mandatory Debt Declaration  
No debt may be created unless all of the following are declared at inception:

- Purpose of the debt  
- Principal amount or range  
- Duration and maturity date  
- Payer class  
- Repayment source  
- Sunset condition  
- Constitutional basis

Debt lacking any declaration is void.


Rule E2 — Duration Requirement  
All debt must include a fixed maturity.

Perpetual debt is prohibited.
Rolling extensions are prohibited.


V. INTERGENERATIONAL CONSTRAINTS

Rule E3 — Consent Boundary  
No debt may impose repayment on a future generation without explicit structural protection.

Unfunded promises are prohibited.
Assumed growth is not consent.


Rule E4 — Prohibition on Unfunded Liabilities  
Programs creating future payment obligations without dedicated funding are invalid.

Promises without assets are void.


VI. BORROWING LIMITS

Rule E5 — Enumerated Purpose Limitation  
Debt may only be issued for purposes explicitly authorized by law and enumerated authority.

Debt for redistribution, administration, or permanent programs is prohibited.


Rule E6 — Debt Scope Containment  
Debt must be node-scoped.

Failure in one node may not be offset by another.
No global absorption of liability.


VII. REPAYMENT AND EXTINCTION

Rule E7 — Mandatory Amortization  
All debt must amortize toward zero.

Interest-only structures are prohibited.
Deferred principal is prohibited.


Rule E8 — Early Retirement Incentive  
Early repayment is permitted and encouraged.

No penalties for early extinction of debt.


VIII. DEBT FAILURE AND DEFAULT

Rule E9 — Failure Resolution  
If repayment conditions fail:
- New borrowing halts automatically
- Authority tied to the debt suspends
- Assets revert according to Property Rights and Pooling Control (00C)

Default resolves to structure, not discretion.


IX. EMERGENCY BORROWING

Rule E10 — Emergency Debt Limitation  
Emergency debt:
- Must declare the emergency
- Must include a shortened maturity
- May not exceed one cycle without reauthorization
- May not convert into permanent obligation

Emergency does not suspend extinction.


X. ENFORCEMENT

Rule E11 — Automatic Invalidity  
Debt issued in violation of this Control is void.

No ratification.
No retroactive justification.


Rule E12 — Personal Liability  
Officials authorizing unlawful debt lose immunity protections.


XI. NON-OVERRIDABILITY

This Control may not be overridden by:
- Emergency declarations
- Budget acts
- Administrative rules
- Judicial delay

Debt discipline is prior to politics.


XII. SYSTEM INTEGRATION

This Control governs:
- National borrowing
- Trust fund obligations
- Guarantees and backstops
- Healthcare and retirement promises
- Any future-dated commitment

No AGCL node is exempt.


XIII. DECLARATION

A nation that borrows without end mortgages its children.

This Control ensures debt ends before liberty does.

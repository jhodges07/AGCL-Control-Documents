00C — Property Rights and Pooling Control
American Gold Contract for Liberty (AGCL)
Status: Foundational Control
Scope: All AGCL-governed assets, accounts, funds, taxes, trusts, benefits, and pooled mechanisms


I. PURPOSE

This Control establishes property rights as prior to governance.

No asset may be pooled before ownership is defined.
No benefit may be distributed without clear title.
No liability may be imposed without consent or exit.

Pooling is permitted only after property rights are explicit, traceable, and reversible.


II. GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

Ownership precedes authority.

Property that is not owned cannot be governed.
Pooling without ownership is confiscation.
Redistribution without title is coercion.


III. DEFINITIONS

Property  
Any asset, income, contribution, account, credit, entitlement, benefit, or claim governed by AGCL, whether monetary or non-monetary.

Owner  
The natural person holding legal title to property, with the right to use, exclude, transfer, inherit, or exit.

Pooling  
Any aggregation of property, funds, risk, revenue, or obligation across multiple owners.

Reversion  
The automatic return of property or value to its owner upon termination, exit, or failure.


IV. OWNERSHIP DECLARATION REQUIREMENTS

Rule C1 — Mandatory Ownership Declaration  
Every node must declare ownership for all property it touches.

Declarations must specify:
- Owner
- Title type
- Transfer conditions
- Inheritance rules
- Exit disposition

Property without declared ownership is invalid for pooling.


Rule C2 — Individual Title Default  
Absent explicit declaration, property is presumed individually owned.

Government bears the burden of proof for any alternative claim.


V. POOLING PERMISSIONS

Rule C3 — Conditional Pooling Only  
Pooling is permitted only if:
- Ownership is explicit prior to pooling
- Participation is voluntary or includes a defined exit
- Pool purpose is limited and declared
- Pool duration is finite
- Reversion rules are specified

All five conditions are mandatory.


Rule C4 — No Involuntary Permanent Pools  
No property may be permanently pooled without ongoing consent.

Silence is not consent.
Continuation is not consent.
Participation must be reaffirmable.


VI. POOL FAILURE AND REVERSION

Rule C5 — Automatic Reversion on Failure  
If a pool fails its purpose, exceeds duration, or violates scope:
- Pooling terminates immediately
- Remaining value reverts to owners
- No cross-subsidization is permitted

Failure resolves to owners, not administrators.


Rule C6 — Prohibition on Liability Transfer  
Owners may not inherit liabilities created by pooled failure beyond their contributed value.

No negative inheritance.
No open-ended claims.


VII. TAXATION AND PROPERTY

Rule C7 — Taxes Do Not Transfer Ownership  
Payment of tax does not transfer title to future income, assets, or labor.

Taxes confer no claim beyond the declared obligation.


Rule C8 — Prohibition on Property Tax Without Title  
No recurring tax may be imposed on owned property absent a corresponding ownership claim by the taxing authority.

Ownership without title is prohibited.


VIII. TRUSTS, FUNDS, AND ACCOUNTS

Rule C9 — Trust Partitioning  
All trusts, lockboxes, and funds must:
- Maintain individual account partitions
- Preserve owner identity
- Prohibit commingling beyond declared pooling

Partitions are inviolable.


Rule C10 — Inheritance Guarantee  
Individually owned property is fully inheritable.

Beneficiaries must be named.
Escheat without consent is prohibited.


IX. GOVERNMENT BORROWING AND USE

Rule C11 — Borrowing Requires Owner Consent  
Use of pooled property for borrowing requires:
- Declared terms
- Declared duration
- Declared repayment source
- Declared owner benefit

Undeclared borrowing is confiscation.


X. ENFORCEMENT

Rule C12 — Automatic Invalidity  
Any action violating ownership or pooling rules is void.

No retroactive cure.
No political ratification.


Rule C13 — Personal Liability  
Officials authorizing unlawful pooling or confiscation lose immunity protections.


XI. NON-OVERRIDABILITY

This Control may not be overridden by:
- Emergency powers
- Budget acts
- Administrative rules
- Judicial delay

Property rights are prior to governance.


XII. SYSTEM INTEGRATION

This Control governs:
- FairTax mechanisms
- Sinking funds
- Retirement systems
- Healthcare accounts
- Public ledgers
- Debt instruments

No AGCL node is exempt.


XIII. DECLARATION

A free system begins with ownership.
A coercive system begins with pooling.

This Control ensures property is never governed before it is owned.

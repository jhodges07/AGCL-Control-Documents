00F — Uncertainty and Black-Swan Stress Control
American Gold Contract for Liberty (AGCL)
Status: Foundational Control
Scope: All AGCL-governed systems exposed to uncertainty, shock, tail risk, or systemic failure


I. PURPOSE

This Control forces systems to acknowledge uncertainty before it destroys them.

No system may assume normal conditions.
No authority may deny the existence of unknowns.
No program may operate without declared failure modes.

Uncertainty ignored becomes catastrophe.


II. GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

Risk can be measured.
Uncertainty cannot.

Systems must therefore be designed to fail safely, degrade gracefully, and terminate automatically when reality diverges from assumptions.


III. DEFINITIONS

Risk  
A known probability distribution of outcomes.

Uncertainty  
Outcomes or conditions that cannot be reliably modeled, forecast, or priced.

Black Swan  
A low-frequency, high-impact event outside modeled expectations that materially alters system assumptions.

Stress Event  
Any condition that invalidates baseline assumptions, cost models, performance metrics, or operational feasibility.


IV. UNCERTAINTY DECLARATION REQUIREMENTS

Rule F1 — Mandatory Uncertainty Disclosure  
Every AGCL node must declare:
- Known risks
- Known unknowns
- Assumptions underlying normal operation
- Conditions under which assumptions fail

Silence constitutes noncompliance.


Rule F2 — Prohibition on Certainty Claims  
No authority may claim comprehensive control, prediction, or foresight.

Claims of certainty invalidate authority.


V. STRESS SCENARIO REQUIREMENTS

Rule F3 — Mandatory Stress Scenarios  
Each node must define stress scenarios, including:
- Economic shock
- Supply disruption
- Political disruption
- Technological failure
- Legal invalidation
- Loss of participation or consent

Scenarios must include worst-case bounds.


Rule F4 — Stress Testing Interval  
Stress scenarios must be tested on a fixed cycle.

Untested scenarios are treated as failures.


VI. FAILURE MODE DECLARATION

Rule F5 — Failure Mode Transparency  
Each node must declare:
- How it fails
- How failure is detected
- How failure is contained
- How failure terminates authority

Failure without declaration triggers suspension.


Rule F6 — Graceful Degradation Requirement  
Systems must degrade in stages, not collapse suddenly.

Cliff-edge designs are prohibited.


VII. AUTOMATIC RESPONSE RULES

Rule F7 — Threshold Triggers  
Predefined thresholds must trigger:
- Automatic downgrade
- Scope reduction
- Funding reduction
- Authority suspension
- Program termination

Discretionary delay is prohibited.


Rule F8 — No Bailout Presumption  
No node may assume rescue, bailout, backstop, or emergency override.

Rescue must be explicitly authorized and time-bound.


VIII. CROSS-NODE CONTAINMENT

Rule F9 — Failure Isolation  
Failure in one node may not propagate by default.

Cross-node contagion requires explicit authorization.


IX. EMERGENCY CONDITIONS

Rule F10 — Emergency Does Not Cancel Uncertainty  
Emergency declarations:
- Do not negate uncertainty
- Do not suspend stress rules
- Do not justify assumption override

Emergency accelerates, it does not excuse.


X. ENFORCEMENT

Rule F11 — Automatic Suspension  
Failure to comply with this Control triggers immediate suspension of authority.

No vote required.
No judicial action required.


Rule F12 — Personal Liability  
Officials who suppress uncertainty disclosures or delay trigger execution lose immunity protections.


XI. NON-OVERRIDABILITY

This Control may not be overridden by:
- Emergency powers
- Budget acts
- Administrative rules
- Judicial delay

Uncertainty is prior to authority.


XII. SYSTEM INTEGRATION

This Control governs:
- Monetary systems
- Taxation systems
- Healthcare systems
- Retirement systems
- Trusts and funds
- Any long-duration program

No AGCL node is exempt.


XIII. DECLARATION

Systems fail not because they face uncertainty,
but because they pretend it does not exist.

This Control ensures uncertainty is surfaced before it becomes fatal.

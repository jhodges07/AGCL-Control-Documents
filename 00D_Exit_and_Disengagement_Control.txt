00D — Exit and Disengagement Control
American Gold Contract for Liberty (AGCL)
Status: Foundational Control
Scope: All AGCL-governed programs, taxes, funds, services, mandates, pooled mechanisms, and authorities


I. PURPOSE

This Control establishes exit as a structural right, not a political request, protest, waiver, or exception.

No system may compel participation without a defined exit.
No benefit may bind a citizen indefinitely.
No authority may trap individuals inside pooled risk or obligation.

Exit is the primary safeguard against abuse of power.


II. GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

Consent without exit is coercion.

A system that cannot be exited is not voluntary.
A benefit without disengagement is a liability.
A right that cannot be exercised is not a right.


III. DEFINITIONS

Exit  
The unconditional right of a citizen to disengage from a government program, tax mechanism, pool, mandate, service, or authority without penalty, retaliation, delay, or loss of unrelated rights.

Disengagement  
The mechanical execution of exit, including settlement, partitioning, reconciliation, and termination of obligations.

Node  
Any discrete AGCL-governed program, fund, trust, service, mandate, or authority.


IV. UNIVERSAL EXIT REQUIREMENT

Rule D1 — Mandatory Exit Path  
Every node must declare an explicit, executable exit path at creation.

A node without a declared exit path is invalid.


Rule D2 — Exit Must Be Mechanical  
Exit paths must be:
- Clearly defined
- Administratively simple
- Time-bounded
- Cost-bounded
- Non-discretionary

Exit may not depend on approval, permission, justification, or ideological compliance.


V. EXIT PROHIBITIONS

Rule D3 — No Exit Penalties  
Exit may not trigger:
- Fines or fees
- Punitive taxes
- Loss of unrelated benefits
- Retaliatory audits or enforcement
- Surveillance escalation
- Blacklisting or reentry punishment


Rule D4 — No Exit Conditioning  
Exit may not be conditioned on:
- Future behavior
- Mandatory counseling
- Waiting periods beyond settlement mechanics
- Political or administrative compliance
- Re-enrollment restrictions


VI. FINANCIAL SETTLEMENT ON EXIT

Rule D5 — Ownership Reconciliation  
Upon exit, all individually owned assets:
- Remain the property of the owner, or
- Are returned to the owner, or
- Are converted according to Property Rights and Pooling Control (00C)

No pooled asset may be confiscated due to exit.


Rule D6 — Liability Closure  
Upon exit:
- All future obligations terminate
- Past obligations settle at declared terms
- No open-ended or contingent liabilities survive


VII. TAXATION AND EXIT

Rule D7 — Tax Exit Compatibility  
Any tax mechanism under AGCL must:
- Permit exit via non-participation or alternative compliance
- Provide visible accounting at exit
- Prohibit retroactive taxation after exit


VIII. SERVICES AND BENEFITS

Rule D8 — Service Decoupling  
Citizens may exit:
- Healthcare systems
- Retirement systems
- Welfare or assistance programs
- Regulatory participation schemes

Exit may not revoke constitutional rights or private market access.


IX. SYSTEM CONTINUITY AND RESTRUCTURING

Rule D9 — Exit Survivability  
Exit rights survive:
- Agency mergers
- Program renaming
- Structural reorganization
- Jurisdictional transfer

No restructuring may extinguish exit.


X. EMERGENCY CONDITIONS

Rule D10 — Emergency Does Not Suspend Exit  
Emergency declarations may alter service delivery.
They may not suspend exit rights.
They may not trap citizens inside emergency authority.


XI. ENFORCEMENT

Rule D11 — Automatic Invalidity  
Any action taken to block, delay, punish, or retaliate against exit is void.


Rule D12 — Burden of Proof  
The burden rests on the authority to prove exit was honored.
Failure defaults in favor of the citizen.


XII. NON-OVERRIDABILITY

This Control may not be overridden by:
- Emergency powers
- Budget acts
- Administrative rules
- Judicial delay
- Inter-agency agreements

Exit is prior to governance.


XIII. SYSTEM INTEGRATION

This Control governs:
- FairTax participation mechanics
- Healthcare system participation
- Retirement and trust systems
- Welfare and assistance programs
- Regulatory and compliance schemes
- Any pooled risk or funding structure

No AGCL node is exempt.


XIV. DECLARATION

The right to leave is the right that keeps all other rights honest.

A system that must trap its citizens to survive deserves to fail.

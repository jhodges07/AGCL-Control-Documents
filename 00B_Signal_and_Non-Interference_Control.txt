00B — Signal and Non-Interference Control
American Gold Contract for Liberty (AGCL)
Status: Canonical System Control
Scope: All AGCL and NBBF-governed information, reporting, measurement, audit, and feedback systems


I. PURPOSE

This Control preserves the integrity of reality-to-authority feedback.

No system may function if information is distorted before it reaches decision-makers.
No authority may suppress, filter, delay, reframe, or sanitize signals in order to preserve itself.

Truth must travel faster than power.


II. GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

Authority must adapt to reality.
Reality must never be adapted to authority.

Any system that interferes with signal destroys its own legitimacy.


III. DEFINITIONS

Signal  
Any data, measurement, report, audit result, variance, outcome, cost figure, performance metric, failure indicator, or citizen feedback generated by a governed system.

Interference  
Any action that alters, suppresses, delays, filters, reframes, aggregates, reclassifies, obscures, or conditions a signal before it reaches its lawful destination.

Signal Path  
The required, direct route a signal must take from source to publication or decision point.


IV. SIGNAL INTEGRITY REQUIREMENTS

Rule B1 — Direct Signal Transmission  
All signals must travel from source to destination without discretionary modification.

No intermediate interpretation layers.
No narrative framing.
No reputational filtering.

Raw signal precedes explanation.


Rule B2 — Mandatory Publication  
All non-classified signals must be published within a defined time window.

Delay is interference.
Silence is interference.
Aggregation that hides variance is interference.


Rule B3 — Separation of Signal and Response  
Entities responsible for producing signals may not control the response to those signals.

Measurement and action must be institutionally separated.


V. PROHIBITED INTERFERENCE

Rule B4 — Suppression Prohibition  
No authority may:
- Suppress unfavorable data
- Delay adverse findings
- Redefine metrics mid-cycle
- Change baselines retroactively
- Bury results in summary documents

Intent is irrelevant.
Effect controls.


Rule B5 — Narrative Substitution Prohibition  
Narrative explanation may not replace raw signal.

Narratives may follow data.
Narratives may not precede data.
Narratives may not contradict data.


VI. VARIANCE HANDLING

Rule B6 — Variance Must Surface  
All variance outside declared tolerances must surface automatically.

No managerial discretion.
No political buffering.
No reputation shielding.


Rule B7 — Variance Cannot Be Pooled  
Variance may not be averaged away, offset, or absorbed by unrelated success.

Each node owns its failure.


VII. CLASSIFICATION AND SECRECY

Rule B8 — Secrecy Limits Detail, Not Existence  
Classified programs must still disclose:
- Existence
- Cost range
- Duration
- Legal authority
- Oversight body

Classification may hide details.
It may not hide existence.


VIII. WHISTLEBLOWER SIGNAL PROTECTION

Rule B9 — Signal Origin Protection  
Any individual transmitting a valid signal is protected from retaliation.

Retaliation constitutes interference.
Interference voids authority.


IX. ENFORCEMENT

Rule B10 — Automatic Invalidity  
Decisions made using suppressed, altered, or delayed signals are invalid.

No cure by later disclosure.
No retroactive justification.


Rule B11 — Personal Liability  
Officials who interfere with signal lose immunity protections related to that action.


X. NON-OVERRIDABILITY

This Control may not be suspended by:
- Emergency declarations
- Executive orders
- Budget acts
- Administrative rulemaking
- Judicial delay

Signal integrity is prior to governance.


XI. SYSTEM INTEGRATION

This Control governs:
- AGCL monetary systems
- Taxation systems
- Budgeting systems (NBBF)
- Trusts, lockboxes, and funds
- Healthcare and welfare reporting
- Enforcement and audit systems

No node is exempt.


XII. DECLARATION

When signal is corrupted, power lies.
When power lies, systems fail.

This Control ensures reality always speaks louder than authority.

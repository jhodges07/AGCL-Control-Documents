00I — Enforcement and Remedy Control
American Gold Contract for Liberty (AGCL)
Status: Foundational Control
Scope: All AGCL-governed authorities, programs, funds, taxes, debts, and enforcement actions


I. PURPOSE

This Control ensures that violations of AGCL structure produce automatic consequences.

No rule is real without enforcement.
No authority is legitimate without remedy.
No system survives if violations carry no cost.

Enforcement must be mechanical, not political.


II. GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

Law without consequence is suggestion.

Authority that cannot be invalidated will expand.
Power that cannot be penalized will repeat abuse.
Remedy must follow violation automatically.


III. DEFINITIONS

Violation  
Any action, omission, delay, concealment, or interference that breaches an AGCL Control.

Invalid Action  
An act taken without lawful authority or in violation of an AGCL Control.

Remedy  
The mandatory corrective outcome imposed by structure following a violation.


IV. AUTOMATIC INVALIDATION

Rule I1 — Immediate Voidance  
Any action taken in violation of an AGCL Control is void ab initio.

No ratification.
No grandfathering.
No retroactive authorization.


Rule I2 — Derivative Nullification  
Actions derived from an invalid action are also void.

Illegality propagates forward, not backward.


V. AUTHORITY SUSPENSION

Rule I3 — Automatic Suspension  
Authority responsible for a violation suspends immediately upon detection.

Suspension requires no vote.
Suspension requires no court order.


Rule I4 — Reinstatement Burden  
Reinstatement requires proof of:
- Full correction
- Structural compliance
- Non-recurrence

Silence equals continued suspension.


VI. PERSONAL CONSEQUENCES

Rule I5 — Immunity Shield Removal  
Officials acting outside lawful authority lose immunity protections for those actions.


Rule I6 — Restitution Obligation  
Victims of invalid actions are entitled to restitution.

Restitution precedes punishment.


VII. REMEDY HIERARCHY

Rule I7 — Priority of Remedy  
Remedies apply in the following order:
1. Invalidation
2. Restitution
3. Suspension
4. Removal from authority
5. Civil or criminal referral (if applicable)

No step may be skipped.


VIII. NO DISCRETION CLAUSE

Rule I8 — Prohibition on Waiver  
No authority may waive enforcement.

No exceptions.
No hardship carve-outs.
No political discretion.


IX. WHISTLEBLOWER AND SIGNAL ENFORCEMENT

Rule I9 — Signal Trigger  
Credible signals of violation automatically initiate enforcement.

Suppression of signals constitutes a separate violation.


X. APPEAL AND REVIEW

Rule I10 — Limited Review  
Review may address:
- Factual accuracy
- Scope of remedy

Review may not delay enforcement.


XI. NON-OVERRIDABILITY

This Control may not be overridden by:
- Emergency declarations
- Executive orders
- Budget acts
- Administrative rules
- Judicial delay

Enforcement is prior to authority.


XII. SYSTEM INTEGRATION

This Control governs:
- All AGCL Controls
- All policy nodes
- All fiscal and monetary actions
- All enforcement mechanisms

No AGCL component is exempt.


XIII. DECLARATION

A system that cannot enforce its limits will lose them.

This Control ensures violations end authority, not debate.

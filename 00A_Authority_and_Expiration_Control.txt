00A — Authority and Expiration Control
American Gold Contract for Liberty (AGCL)
Status: Foundational Control
Scope: All AGCL-governed authority, programs, funds, offices, mechanisms, and delegated powers


I. PURPOSE

This Control establishes authority mortality as a governing principle of the AGCL system.

No authority may exist by inertia.
No power may persist without proof.
No program may survive its purpose.

Authority exists only to solve a defined problem, for a defined duration, under defined performance constraints, and expires automatically when those conditions are not met.


II. GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

All authority is conditional, temporary, and revocable by structure.

Authority is not legitimacy.
Authority is not permanence.
Authority is a tool with a timer.


III. DEFINITIONS

Authority  
Any legal, fiscal, regulatory, administrative, monetary, or enforcement power exercised under AGCL, including but not limited to programs, boards, trusts, funds, agencies, mandates, rules, or delegated functions.

Authorization Window  
The fixed period during which authority is valid.

Reauthorization Event  
A mandatory review at the close of each authorization window.

Variance  
Any material deviation from declared purpose, cost range, scope, duration, or constitutional basis.


IV. AUTHORITY CREATION REQUIREMENTS

No authority may be created unless all of the following are explicitly declared at inception:

1. Purpose Statement  
   The specific problem the authority exists to solve.

2. Scope Boundary  
   What the authority may and may not do.

3. Duration  
   A fixed expiration date or cycle count.

4. Performance Metrics  
   Observable criteria by which success or failure is measured.

5. Exit Condition  
   The condition under which the authority terminates early.

6. Constitutional Basis  
   The enumerated power or amendment permitting the authority.

Authorities lacking any required declaration are null and void.


V. AUTHORITY EXPIRATION RULES

Rule A1 — Mandatory Expiration  
All authority expires automatically at the end of its authorization window.

No extensions by default.  
No implied continuity.  
No emergency rollover.

Rule A2 — Reauthorization Burden  
Reauthorization requires affirmative action.

Silence equals expiration.  
Delay equals expiration.  
Failure to vote equals expiration.

Rule A3 — Performance Failure Termination  
Authority terminates immediately if:
- Performance metrics are unmet, or
- Variance exceeds declared tolerance, or
- Purpose is achieved, or
- Purpose becomes obsolete.

Rule A4 — Emergency Authority Limitation  
Emergency authority:
- Must declare an emergency condition
- Must specify a shortened authorization window
- May not exceed one cycle without reauthorization
- May not convert into permanent authority

Emergency does not suspend expiration.


VI. VARIANCE AND SUSPENSION

Rule A5 — Automatic Suspension  
Any unresolved variance triggers immediate suspension of authority until corrected.

Suspension requires no vote.  
Suspension requires no judicial action.  
Suspension is mechanical.

Rule A6 — Scope Creep Prohibition  
Expansion of authority scope without reauthorization is prohibited.

Unauthorized expansion results in:
- Immediate suspension
- Mandatory review
- Retroactive invalidation of excess actions


VII. DELEGATION AND SUB-AUTHORITY

Rule A7 — Delegation Inherits Mortality  
Delegated authority:
- Inherits the expiration date of the parent authority
- Cannot outlive the originating authority
- Cannot expand scope independently

No orphaned power is permitted.


VIII. FUNDING AND AUTHORITY LINKAGE

Rule A8 — Funding Does Not Create Authority  
Appropriations do not legitimize authority.

If authority expires:
Funding halts automatically.

Rule A9 — Unspent Funds on Expiration  
Upon expiration:
- Unspent funds revert according to Property Rights and Pooling Control (00C)
- No funds may be repurposed without reauthorization


IX. ENFORCEMENT

Rule A10 — Automatic Invalidity  
Actions taken under expired or suspended authority are legally void.

No ratification after the fact.  
No retroactive forgiveness.

Rule A11 — Personal Liability Shield Removal  
Officials acting under expired authority lose immunity protections for those actions.


X. NON-OVERRIDABILITY

This Control may not be suspended, bypassed, or overridden by:
- Emergency declarations
- Executive orders
- Budget acts
- Judicial delay
- Administrative rulemaking

Any attempt to bypass this Control invalidates the action.


XI. SYSTEM INTEGRATION

This Control governs and supersedes:
- All AGCL policy nodes
- All fiscal and monetary mechanisms
- All trusts, lockboxes, and funds
- All regulatory or enforcement authority

No AGCL component is exempt.


XII. DECLARATION

Authority exists to serve reality.
When authority outlives reality, liberty collapses.

This Control ensures authority dies before liberty does.

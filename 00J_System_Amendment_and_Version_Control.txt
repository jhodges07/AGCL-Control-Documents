00J — System Amendment and Version Control
American Gold Contract for Liberty (AGCL)
Status: Foundational Control
Scope: All AGCL control documents, policy nodes, mechanisms, and future revisions


I. PURPOSE

This Control prevents silent mutation of the AGCL system.

No system remains free if it changes invisibly.
No authority may evolve without consent.
No structure may drift by accumulation.

Change must be explicit, recorded, and bounded.


II. GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

Governance without version control becomes arbitrary.

What cannot be versioned cannot be trusted.
What cannot be audited cannot be consented to.
What changes without record ceases to be law.


III. DEFINITIONS

Amendment  
Any modification, addition, deletion, or reinterpretation of an AGCL Control or policy node.

Version  
A uniquely identified state of the AGCL system at a specific point in time.

Change Log  
A permanent public record of all amendments, including rationale and scope.


IV. VERSIONING REQUIREMENTS

Rule J1 — Mandatory Version Identification  
All AGCL documents must display:
- Version number
- Adoption date
- Superseded version (if applicable)

Undated or unversioned documents are invalid.


Rule J2 — Immutable Historical Record  
Prior versions may not be altered or erased.

Corrections require new versions.
History is additive, not replaceable.


V. AMENDMENT PROCEDURE

Rule J3 — Explicit Amendment Requirement  
No change is valid unless:
- Explicitly proposed
- Explicitly documented
- Explicitly adopted

Implicit change is void.


Rule J4 — Scope Declaration  
Each amendment must declare:
- Affected documents
- Sections altered
- Intended effect
- Unintended risks

Undeclared scope invalidates the amendment.


VI. PUBLIC DISCLOSURE

Rule J5 — Mandatory Publication  
All amendments must be published in the public ledger prior to taking effect.

Secret amendments are void.


Rule J6 — Notice Interval  
Amendments require a defined notice period before activation.

Retroactive amendments are prohibited.


VII. COMPATIBILITY AND CASCADE CHECKS

Rule J7 — Control Compatibility Review  
No amendment may:
- Contradict existing Controls
- Weaken enforcement
- Bypass exit rights
- Extend authority duration implicitly

Conflicting amendments are void.


Rule J8 — Cascade Impact Declaration  
Amendments must disclose downstream effects on:
- Authority
- Property rights
- Exit mechanics
- Debt obligations

Silence constitutes noncompliance.


VIII. EMERGENCY AMENDMENTS

Rule J9 — Emergency Limitation  
Emergency amendments:
- Must be narrowly scoped
- Must include an expiration
- May not alter foundational Controls (00A–00I)

Emergency does not permit permanence.


IX. ENFORCEMENT

Rule J10 — Automatic Invalidity  
Amendments adopted outside this Control are void.


Rule J11 — Personal Liability  
Officials who enact or enforce invalid amendments lose immunity protections.


X. NON-OVERRIDABILITY

This Control may not be overridden by:
- Emergency declarations
- Budget acts
- Administrative rules
- Judicial delay

Process is prior to power.


XI. SYSTEM INTEGRATION

This Control governs:
- All AGCL Controls
- All policy nodes
- All future system evolution

No component is exempt.


XII. DECLARATION

A free system remembers its changes.

This Control ensures AGCL evolves by consent, not drift.

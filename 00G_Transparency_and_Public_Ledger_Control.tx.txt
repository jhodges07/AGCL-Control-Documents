00G — Transparency and Public Ledger Control
American Gold Contract for Liberty (AGCL)
Status: Foundational Control
Scope: All AGCL-governed programs, authorities, funds, trusts, taxes, debts, and enforcement actions


I. PURPOSE

This Control guarantees public visibility into the existence, scope, cost, duration, and authority of all AGCL-governed activity.

Transparency is not optional.
Opacity is not neutral.
Hidden authority is illegitimate authority.


II. GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

Citizens may be governed only by what they can see.

Secrecy may protect details.
It may not protect existence.
It may not protect authority.
It may not protect cost.


III. DEFINITIONS

Public Ledger  
A continuously updated, publicly accessible record of AGCL-governed nodes and actions.

Node  
Any discrete program, authority, fund, trust, mandate, tax mechanism, debt instrument, or enforcement power governed by AGCL.

Disclosure  
The publication of required information in the public ledger.


IV. MANDATORY DISCLOSURE REQUIREMENTS

Rule G1 — Existence Disclosure  
Every node must disclose, at minimum:
- Legal name and identifier
- Purpose
- Authorizing authority
- Start date
- Expiration date

Nodes without existence disclosure are invalid.


Rule G2 — Cost and Exposure Disclosure  
Each node must disclose:
- Cost range
- Funding source
- Financial exposure
- Debt or guarantee involvement

Exact figures may be delayed.
Ranges may not be hidden.


Rule G3 — Authority and Scope Disclosure  
Each node must disclose:
- Scope boundaries
- Enforcement powers
- Delegated authorities
- Exit mechanics

Undisclosed authority is void.


V. TIME AND STATUS DISCLOSURE

Rule G4 — Status Signaling  
The public ledger must display:
- Active
- Suspended
- Expired
- Terminated
- Under review

Status must update automatically.


Rule G5 — Duration Visibility  
Remaining duration must be visible at all times.

Countdown is mandatory.
Silence is interference.


VI. CLASSIFICATION AND SECRECY

Rule G6 — Secrecy Boundary  
Classification may limit:
- Operational detail
- Tactical methods
- Personal identifiers

Classification may not limit:
- Existence
- Cost range
- Duration
- Authorizing authority
- Oversight body


VII. LEDGER INTEGRITY

Rule G7 — Tamper Resistance  
Public ledger entries must be immutable once published.

Corrections require:
- Additive entries
- Version history
- Public timestamping


Rule G8 — Independent Replication  
Ledger data must be independently replicable.

Single-point custody is prohibited.


VIII. ACCESSIBILITY

Rule G9 — Universal Access  
The public ledger must be:
- Freely accessible
- Machine-readable
- Human-readable
- Searchable

No paywalls.
No credentials.
No gatekeepers.


IX. ENFORCEMENT

Rule G10 — Automatic Invalidity  
Actions taken under undisclosed or misrepresented authority are void.


Rule G11 — Personal Liability  
Officials who conceal, falsify, or delay disclosure lose immunity protections.


X. NON-OVERRIDABILITY

This Control may not be overridden by:
- Emergency declarations
- Executive orders
- Budget acts
- Administrative rules
- Judicial delay

Transparency is prior to trust.


XI. SYSTEM INTEGRATION

This Control governs:
- Budgetary activity
- Monetary operations
- Trusts and lockboxes
- Taxation systems
- Enforcement actions
- All AGCL authorities

No node is exempt.


XII. DECLARATION

What cannot be seen cannot be consented to.

This Control ensures authority remains visible, finite, and accountable.

# Point 08 — National Jury Reform Amendment

## Purpose

Restore impartial federal justice by restructuring jury selection, authority, and protection so that juries function as an independent constitutional check on government power, free from regional bias, political capture, and economic coercion.

---

## Core Structure

Federal juries are drawn from the full body of eligible U.S. citizens, not from local districts.

* No regional concentration of jurors
* No political or demographic stacking
* No local court capture

Jurors represent the nation as a whole, not a jurisdictional subset.

---

## National Jury Pool

All federal grand and trial juries are selected by lot from a national pool.

* Randomized selection process
* Nationwide eligibility
* No district-based limitation

This eliminates geographic bias and ensures distributed representation.

---

## One Juror Per State

Each juror must represent a different State.

* No duplication of state representation
* Prevents regional dominance
* Distributes influence across the country

No single state or region can control jury outcomes.

---

## Eligibility Requirements

Jurors must meet defined civic criteria:

* Natural-born U.S. citizen
* Participation in at least one federal election
* Legal ownership of real property within the United States

Jury service is tied to demonstrated civic participation and long-term national stake.

---

## Population Quintile Representation

Juries must reflect national population distribution.

* Representation from rural, suburban, and urban populations
* Balanced across census-based population tiers

This ensures that no single demographic dominates deliberation.

---

## Compensation and Financial Independence

Jurors receive compensation equal to the presiding federal judge for the duration of service.

* Full travel and lodging provided
* No financial burden or loss of income

This removes economic pressure and ensures independence in decision-making.

---

## Security and Protection

Jurors are protected by the United States Marshals Service.

* Full protection during service
* Shielded from intimidation or coercion
* Enforcement of juror safety as a federal obligation

Jurors operate free from external influence.

---

## Independent Jury Authority

Juries possess full constitutional authority to evaluate:

* The facts of the case
* The conduct and impartiality of the presiding judge
* The constitutionality of the law or regulation being applied

Juries may invalidate applications of law that violate constitutional authority.

---

## Congressional Implementation

Congress must establish:

* Random selection systems
* Eligibility verification procedures
* Population balancing methods
* Summoning and service protocols
* Hardship exemptions
* Juror protection standards

All systems must operate under transparency and enforceability requirements.

---

## Enforcement Structure

* Jury selection rules execute automatically through defined systems
* No judicial or administrative override permitted
* Violations invalidate jury formation and proceedings

Compliance is structural, not discretionary.

---

## Case Illustration

A family of four involved in a federal case:

**Current System**

* Jury drawn from a single district
* Potential political or demographic bias
* Limited ability to challenge judicial conduct
* Economic burden may discourage full participation

**Under National Jury Reform**

* Jury composed of citizens from across the country
* Balanced representation across states and populations
* Jurors compensated fully, removing financial pressure
* Jury may evaluate both the law and its application
* Proceedings are insulated from local influence

---

## Outcome

* Eliminates regional and political jury manipulation
* Restores juries as a constitutional check on government
* Ensures national representation in federal trials
* Protects juror independence through compensation and security
* Reinforces constitutional limits on law and enforcement

The jury becomes the final authority between the citizen and the state, operating independently, nationally, and constitutionally.

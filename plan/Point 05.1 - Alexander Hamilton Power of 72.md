# Point 05.1 — Alexander Hamilton Power of 72 Sinking Liberty Fund

## Purpose

Establish a constitutionally disciplined, individually owned capital system that converts taxpayers into creditors of the nation, eliminates permanent deficit financing, and aligns national solvency with citizen wealth.

This framework restores Hamiltonian public credit through ownership, limits, and enforced repayment.

---

## Core Structure

Each citizen is issued an individually owned Sinking Liberty Fund.

* Funded automatically from federal tax payments
* Recorded on a public blockchain (government traceable, citizen anonymous)
* Fully inheritable with mandatory beneficiaries
* Protected from seizure, liens, judgments, bankruptcy, or civil claims

The fund is private property. The government has no ownership claim.

---

## Contribution Mechanism

Up to 25% of all federal taxes paid are automatically credited to the individual’s fund.

* 5% permanent base floor at all times
* Maximum ceiling of 25%
* Adjustments are reduction-only

If no valid reduction action occurs, the contribution rate increases automatically by 1 percentage point per month until the 25% ceiling is reached.

All rate changes require recorded roll-call votes within defined time windows.

---

## Permanent Principal Rule

A fixed portion of all contributions becomes permanent principal.

* May never be withdrawn
* May never be spent
* May only be lent under defined rules

This principal anchors long-term national credit and prevents capital erosion.

---

## Borrowing Framework

### Treasury Priority Borrowing

The United States Treasury must borrow from citizen funds first.

* Borrowing rates capped at half of prevailing market rates
* All interest is credited directly to the citizen’s account
* All principal must be repaid on schedule

No discretionary borrowing channels exist outside this system.

---

### Bank and Credit Union Lending

Only American-owned FDIC banks and credit unions may access these funds.

* 75% of returns credited to the citizen
* 25% distributed to federal, state, and local governments

Prohibited activities:

* Derivatives
* Leverage structures
* Foreign participation

All lending must remain transparent and bounded.

---

## Income and Access Options

Account holders may elect income or compounding.

* Up to 33% of monthly yield may be withdrawn
* Remaining yield compounds automatically

When yield exceeds defined thresholds:

* Up to 95% of yield may be accessed
* Lending or borrowing for family members becomes available through regulated institutions

All access is controlled by the account holder, not Congress.

---

## Constitutional Discipline Rules

### Surplus Reversion

All unspent federal funds are returned to the citizen’s account.

Government surplus is not retained.

---

### Deficit Elimination

Deficit spending is prohibited.

* All existing debt must be serviced
* Federal spending must decline annually until debt reaches zero

---

### Enumerated Authority Budgeting

All federal spending must be classified:

* Enumerated
* Non-enumerated

Non-enumerated spending:

* Cannot be debt financed
* Cannot access permanent principal
* Expires automatically unless reauthorized

---

### Budget Failure Enforcement

If Congress fails to pass a lawful budget:

* The Executive must reduce or eliminate non-compliant spending
* Priority is given to eliminating non-enumerated programs

---

## Absolute Borrowing Limits

* No borrowing from foreign governments
* No borrowing from the Federal Reserve
* No sale of Treasury bonds to foreign interests

All national borrowing is internal and citizen-based.

---

## Case Illustration

A family of four paying federal taxes:

**Current System**

* Pays taxes with no ownership claim
* Savings depend on external financial markets
* National debt grows without direct benefit to the family
* Foreign and institutional creditors control national financing

**Under the Sinking Liberty Fund**

* A defined portion of taxes becomes personal capital
* Funds grow through interest and compounding
* The family becomes a creditor to the United States
* National borrowing strengthens household wealth
* No exposure to foreign-controlled debt structures

---

## Outcome

* Eliminates permanent deficit financing
* Replaces foreign debt with citizen-owned capital
* Builds generational wealth automatically
* Enforces constitutional limits on spending
* Aligns government stability with citizen prosperity

Public credit becomes disciplined, finite, and owned by the people.

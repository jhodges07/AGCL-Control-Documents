# Point 07 — No CBDC and Full Blockchain Accountability

## Purpose

Prohibit the creation of any Central Bank Digital Currency and replace opaque government finance with full, non-discretionary blockchain accountability that preserves citizen privacy while enforcing complete transparency of government activity.

---

## Absolute Prohibition of Central Bank Digital Currency

No Central Bank Digital Currency may be created, issued, tested, or implemented.

* No programmable currency under government control
* No transaction-level monitoring of citizens
* No conditional spending restrictions
* No delayed, denied, or prioritized transactions

Any attempt to establish a CBDC is invalid and unenforceable.

Money must remain a neutral medium of exchange, not a behavioral control mechanism.

---

## Replacement Principle

Secrecy within government is eliminated. Privacy for citizens is preserved.

* Government activity becomes fully transparent
* Citizen financial behavior remains anonymous
* No surveillance systems are permitted

Transparency applies only to public authority, never to private individuals.

---

## Blockchain Accountability System

All federal revenue and spending is recorded on a unified public blockchain.

Each tax dollar is assigned a unique identifier at the point of collection.

From that moment forward, the dollar is traceable through all government activity.

Recorded data includes:

* Point of collection
* Allocation pathway
* Receiving agency
* Program utilization
* Authorizing legislation
* Constitutional authority cited
* Legislative votes and approvals
* Final disposition

All three branches of government are included without exception.

No agency, contractor, or intermediary is excluded.

---

## Citizen Privacy Protection

* No transaction data is linked to individuals
* No consumption tracking
* No identity-based profiling
* Only aggregate tax contributions are recorded

The system enforces full government visibility with zero citizen surveillance.

---

## Taxpayer-Controlled Spending Exposure

Each taxpayer establishes a standing instruction governing the use of their tax dollars.

Two options are available:

### Enumerated Authority Only

* Tax dollars may be used solely for constitutionally authorized functions
* All non-enumerated allocations are automatically blocked

---

### Non-Enumerated Spending Opt-Out

Funds assigned to non-enumerated uses are automatically redirected:

* 75% applied to national debt reduction
* 25% credited to the taxpayer’s Sinking Liberty Fund

No administrative override or exception is permitted.

---

## Enforcement Structure

* All allocation rules execute automatically at the ledger level
* No discretionary routing of funds
* Any attempt to bypass the system invalidates the transaction

Government compliance is mandatory and continuously verifiable.

---

## Integration with AGCL System

This framework reinforces:

* Taxpayer supremacy over public funds
* Enumerated authority enforcement
* Debt reduction acceleration
* Individual capital formation through redirection

It eliminates hidden spending pathways and forces structural compliance.

---

## Case Illustration

A family of four paying federal taxes:

**Current System**

* No visibility into how tax dollars are spent
* Funds may support programs outside constitutional authority
* No ability to restrict or redirect usage
* Government operates through opaque budgeting systems

**Under Blockchain Accountability**

* Every tax dollar is traceable through government use
* Family may restrict funds to enumerated authority
* Non-authorized spending is automatically redirected
* No surveillance of family financial activity occurs
* Government actions are fully visible and auditable

---

## Outcome

* Eliminates financial surveillance systems
* Ends opaque and discretionary budgeting
* Restores constitutional limits on spending
* Protects citizen privacy absolutely
* Converts unauthorized spending into debt reduction and personal capital

Transparency applies to government.
Privacy applies to the citizen.
Control is enforced through structure, not permission.

# Point 06 — Labor Sovereignty and Wage Restoration

## Purpose

Restore American labor markets to constitutional order by eliminating wage suppression mechanisms, ending foreign labor substitution, and enforcing fair market pricing of domestic work.

Labor is treated as a domestic economic resource governed under constitutional commerce authority, not a global arbitrage input.

---

## Core Structure

The United States labor market is reserved for U.S. citizens and lawful domestic participants.

* No external labor pipelines permitted
* No artificial wage suppression mechanisms allowed
* No substitution of domestic labor through foreign sourcing

Labor pricing is determined by domestic supply and demand without distortion.

---

## Termination of Federal Work-Visa Systems

All federal programs authorizing non-citizen labor participation are terminated.

* No new work visas issued
* No renewals or extensions permitted
* No administrative or legislative exemptions allowed

Labor access is not an entitlement of corporations and is withdrawn where it conflicts with national economic stability.

---

## Prohibition on Labor Substitution

Employers may not replace or bypass domestic workers using foreign labor mechanisms.

* Applies to direct hiring, contracting, and third-party arrangements
* Applies equally to physical and digital labor substitution

Intentional replacement of domestic labor constitutes a violation of federal labor sovereignty.

---

## Remote Labor as Imported Service

All labor performed outside the United States for domestic benefit is classified as an imported service.

* A 50% tariff is applied to the gross value of the service
* Applied at invoicing, payment, or settlement
* Applies to:

  * Direct contractors
  * Offshore subsidiaries
  * Third-party providers
  * Platform-based labor

No exemptions based on company size, industry, or labor claims are permitted.

---

## Enforcement Structure

Compliance is enforced through automatic and escalating penalties.

* Financial penalties not less than three times avoided tariffs
* Personal liability for executive officers in cases of willful violation
* Suspension of access to U.S. commercial infrastructure for repeat violations

Enforcement is non-discretionary and system-based.

---

## Constitutional Authority

This policy operates under enumerated federal powers.

* Regulation of foreign commerce includes imported services
* Taxing authority permits tariffs and duties to regulate markets
* Protection of domestic labor markets is a legitimate federal function

No constitutional right exists to import labor or arbitrage global wage differences.

---

## Integration with Revenue System

Labor sovereignty reinforces the FairTax and tariff-based revenue structure.

* Higher domestic wages increase consumption capacity
* Expanded consumption strengthens transaction-based revenue
* Service tariffs restore parity between goods and services

Revenue flows into the federal pool without expanding administrative systems.

---

## Market Effects

* Eliminates wage suppression through foreign labor substitution
* Forces employers to compete for domestic workers
* Aligns wages with domestic cost of living
* Reduces dependency on debt-financed consumption

Labor markets become internally balanced and self-correcting.

---

## Case Illustration

A family of four with two working adults:

**Current System**

* Wages suppressed by foreign labor competition
* Job displacement through outsourcing and visa programs
* Limited wage growth despite productivity
* Increasing reliance on debt to maintain living standards

**Under Labor Sovereignty**

* Employers compete for domestic workers
* Wages rise to reflect true market demand
* Job opportunities expand within the domestic economy
* Household income supports consumption without debt reliance

---

## Outcome

* Restores domestic labor pricing power
* Eliminates artificial wage suppression
* Aligns employment with national economic interest
* Reinforces constitutional control over commerce
* Strengthens middle-class economic stability

Labor is no longer subject to global arbitrage. It is governed within constitutional limits and domestic market discipline.

# Point 02 — FairTax State & Local Revenue Sharing Framework

## Purpose

Establish a non-discretionary, transparent, and control-compliant system for distributing federal consumption-based revenue to states and local governments following the passage of H.R. 25 (FairTax Act).

This framework eliminates state and local dependence on income and property taxation while enforcing structural compliance with AGCL controls.

---

## Federal Revenue Pool Definition

All federal consumption-based revenues are deposited into a single, transparent, auditable pool.

**Included:**

* National sales tax under H.R. 25
* Tariff revenues (where applicable)
* Retailer non-compliance penalties

**Excluded:**

* Income taxes
* Payroll taxes
* Corporate taxes
* Property taxes
* Any IRS-style reporting or assessment systems

A fixed statutory percentage is permanently reserved for state and local distribution. This allocation is formula-driven, automatic, and not subject to Congressional discretion or annual appropriations.

---

## State Qualification Standard (All-In / All-Out)

Participation is binary. A state either qualifies fully or is excluded.

To qualify, a state must permanently eliminate:

* Personal income taxes
* Corporate income taxes
* Payroll taxes
* Capital gains taxes
* Estate and inheritance taxes
* Gross receipts taxes
* Property taxes of any kind

This applies to all state and local jurisdictions without exception.

No equivalent or proxy tax structures may be introduced under alternate naming or mechanisms.

---

## Limited Tax Exceptions (Fixed and Non-Expandable)

**Motor Fuel Excise Taxes**

* Permitted strictly for transportation infrastructure
* No expansion into tracking, surveillance, or proxy taxation

**Legacy Activity Fees**

* Existing fees (e.g., hunting, fishing) may remain
* Locked to rolling 10-year historical averages
* No increases, indexing, or expansion permitted
* No new fee categories allowed

These exceptions are fixed and cannot evolve into broader taxation systems.

---

## Certification and Automatic Enforcement

Each participating state must submit an annual certification confirming:

* All prohibited taxes remain eliminated
* No proxy or equivalent taxation exists
* All exceptions remain within defined limits

Verification is mechanical and publicly auditable.

Failure results in immediate and automatic removal from revenue sharing.

---

## Revenue Distribution Formula

### Geographic Allocation

**Small and Mid-Sized Businesses**

* 50% to seller jurisdiction
* 50% to purchaser jurisdiction

**Large National and Online Retailers**

* 100% to purchaser jurisdiction

---

### Federal / State / Local Split

* 33⅓% Federal
* 33⅓% State
* 33⅓% Local

Retailer compensation authorized under H.R. 25 is excluded from this distribution.

---

## State and Local Sales Tax Authority

States and local governments may levy a sales tax only if fully aligned with H.R. 25 structure:

* Applied only to new goods and consumer services
* Collected at point of sale
* Fully visible and itemized
* No income-based exemptions or thresholds
* No credits, offsets, or behavioral conditions
* No use as proxy for income, payroll, or property taxation

---

## Blockchain Transparency and Audit System

All participating jurisdictions must operate within a unified blockchain system.

* Each tax dollar receives a unique identifier at collection
* Funds are traceable through allocation and use
* Applies equally to federal, state, and local levels
* No taxpayer identity is attached

Failure to comply results in automatic disqualification.

---

## Debt Containment Integration

A defined portion of all revenues is automatically allocated to structured debt reduction or sinking fund mechanisms.

* No open-ended liabilities permitted
* No intergenerational transfer beyond defined schedules
* All obligations must terminate within fixed timelines

---

## Privacy Enforcement

* No tracking of purchased goods
* No linkage to individuals or households
* No consumption profiling
* Only total transaction tax is recorded

Receipts may be retained temporarily for merchant reconciliation only. No consumer audit authority exists.

---

## Enforcement Boundary

* Enforcement applies only at the retailer level
* Compliance is mathematical and system-based
* No investigative or surveillance mechanisms permitted

---

## Clawback and Reversion

Any violation triggers:

* Immediate termination of revenue sharing
* Automatic reallocation of funds to:

  * national debt reduction, or
  * sinking fund structures

No discretionary enforcement is permitted.

---

## Federal Non-Interference

* No conditional grants
* No federal mandates tied to funding
* No behavioral or policy coercion

States retain sovereignty within the constraints of participation.

---

## Case Illustration

A family of four in a qualifying state:

**Current System**

* Pays state income tax
* Pays property tax annually
* Pays hidden taxes embedded in prices
* Cannot exit state tax systems without relocation

**Under H.R. 25 + Revenue Sharing**

* No state income tax
* No property tax
* Pays tax only at point of consumption
* Receives indirect benefit from revenue sharing to local services
* Full visibility of tax paid

**Under Full AGCL System**

* All taxation is transaction-based and visible
* No recurring claims on property or income
* State funding is formula-driven and non-political
* Exit from tax exposure occurs through consumption choices
* No hidden or embedded taxation exists

---

## Outcome

* Eliminates state and local income and property taxation
* Replaces political funding allocation with automatic formulas
* Enforces transparency across all government levels
* Prevents tax creep structurally
* Restores federalism through non-coercive revenue sharing

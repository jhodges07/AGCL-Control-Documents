00H — Constitutional Compatibility Control
American Gold Contract for Liberty (AGCL)
Status: Foundational Control
Scope: All AGCL-governed authorities, programs, funds, taxes, debts, enforcement actions, and delegated powers


I. PURPOSE

This Control ensures that all AGCL mechanisms operate strictly within constitutional authority.

No program may exceed enumerated powers.
No necessity claim may replace legality.
No convenience may override constitutional limits.

If authority cannot be traced to the Constitution, it does not exist.


II. GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

Structure precedes intent.

Good outcomes do not legalize bad authority.
Emergencies do not create powers.
Silence does not imply permission.


III. DEFINITIONS

Enumerated Authority  
A power explicitly granted to the federal government by the United States Constitution.

Non-Delegation  
The principle that Congress may not transfer its lawmaking authority to agencies or external entities.

Compatibility  
Conformity with constitutional text, structure, and binding amendments.


IV. AUTHORITY TRACEABILITY REQUIREMENT

Rule H1 — Mandatory Constitutional Citation  
Every AGCL node must explicitly cite:
- The constitutional article
- The specific section and clause
- Any relevant amendment

Absent citation renders the node invalid.


Rule H2 — No Implied Powers  
Authority may not be inferred from:
- General welfare language
- Administrative precedent
- Historical practice
- Judicial tolerance

Only explicit grants apply.


V. NON-DELEGATION ENFORCEMENT

Rule H3 — Delegation Prohibition  
Legislative authority may not be delegated to:
- Administrative agencies
- Independent boards
- NGOs or private entities
- International bodies

Execution may be delegated.
Authority may not.


Rule H4 — Rulemaking Limits  
Rules may implement law.
They may not create law.

Rules exceeding statutory authority are void.


VI. DUE PROCESS AND PROPERTY PROTECTION

Rule H5 — Due Process Requirement  
No AGCL mechanism may deprive a citizen of:
- Life
- Liberty
- Property

Absent due process of law.


Rule H6 — Property Rights Compatibility  
Any action affecting property must comply with:
- Takings Clause
- Just compensation
- Clear title rules

Administrative seizure is prohibited.


VII. TAXATION AND SPENDING COMPATIBILITY

Rule H7 — Tax Authority Limitation  
Taxes must be:
- Explicitly authorized
- Uniform within scope
- Non-retroactive

Hidden or indirect taxation is prohibited.


Rule H8 — Spending Limitation  
Spending must align with enumerated authority.

Redistribution without explicit constitutional authorization is prohibited.


VIII. JUDICIAL AND LEGAL CONFLICT HANDLING

Rule H9 — Conflict Surfacing  
Any constitutional conflict must be surfaced immediately.

Suppression of conflict is interference.


Rule H10 — Severability Requirement  
Unconstitutional components are severed automatically.

Remaining lawful components may continue.


IX. ENFORCEMENT

Rule H11 — Automatic Invalidity  
Any action lacking constitutional authority is void.


Rule H12 — Personal Liability  
Officials acting outside constitutional bounds lose immunity protections.


X. NON-OVERRIDABILITY

This Control may not be overridden by:
- Emergency declarations
- Budget acts
- Administrative rules
- Judicial delay

The Constitution is prior to governance.


XI. SYSTEM INTEGRATION

This Control governs:
- All AGCL control documents
- All policy nodes
- All fiscal and monetary systems
- All enforcement actions

No AGCL component is exempt.


XII. DECLARATION

The Constitution is not a suggestion.

This Control ensures AGCL operates as law, not aspiration.
